# The Initiators

OpenET is initiated by Shreshth Saxena and Dr. Lauren Fink from [BEATLab](http://beatlab.mcmaster.ca/) at McMaster University, Canada. It builds on their years of experience designing and conducting laboratory and remote eye-tracking studies using [desktop](https://www.mdpi.com/1995-8692/11/2/18), [mobile](https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1145/3706598.3713910), and [webcam-based](https://link.springer.com/article/10.3758/s13428-023-02190-6) systems. The initiative emerged from both thier own research challenges and ongoing conversations with researchers, developers, and practitioners across the eye-tracking community at venues such as ACM ETRA, SIGCHI, ECEM, VSS, and ECVP.

The public release of OpenET will take place with an accepted presentation at [HCII 2026](https://2026.hci.international/) in Montreal, Canada. We are looking forward to introduce the initiative to the wider research community, start new conversations, and invite researchers, developers, and practitioners to help shape OpenET as it grows.
